## 🚨 Problem

The rise of **deepfakes** and **synthetic media** has created serious threats:

- Political misinformation & election interference  
- Corporate fraud & impersonation attacks  
- Digital evidence tampering  
- Identity theft & reputational damage  

Most detection systems today are:

❌ Single-domain  
❌ Black-box  
❌ Vulnerable to adversarial tricks  
❌ Not legally reliable  

---

## ✅ Solution — VERIFAI

**VERIFAI** is a **multi-agent forensic AI platform** that verifies the authenticity of:

📸 Images  
🎥 Videos  
🎧 Audio  

It combines **independent AI agents**, **probabilistic decision logic**, and an **explainability-first design**.

---

## 🧠 Core Innovations

✨ **Multi-Agent Forensic Architecture**  
Multiple specialized agents analyze different evidence sources.

✨ **Cross-Modal Reasoning**  
Correlates visual, audio, temporal, and metadata signals.

✨ **Explainability-First AI**  
Heatmaps • Anomaly timelines • Confidence scores

✨ **Deterministic Decision Engine**  
LLMs assist interpretation — never final verdicts.
